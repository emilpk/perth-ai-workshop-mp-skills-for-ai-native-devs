The Scenario

A client, a legacy shop, and a vibe-coded rewrite

A decade-old C# online store, run by commercial people, not engineers. They'd heard about vibe coding, knew a bit of Python, and used it to build a modern alternative.

🟢 It mostly works

Catalog, basket, checkout, and orders all function — the styling investment is real and worth keeping.

🔴 But it's buggy

They won't rewrite from scratch. They've hired us to fix what's broken and set up guardrails for next time.

Introducing the Approach

Matt Pocock's skills for Claude Code

Structured, reusable workflows that sit on top of the model — designed to catch exactly the failure modes we just talked about.

Helps

Forces understanding before code, gives review a fixed point to check against, and turns "vibing" into a repeatable process.

Gaps

Doesn't replace judgement about scope, and a skill is only as good as the person choosing when to invoke it.
